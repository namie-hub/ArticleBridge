ArticleBridge — Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

ArticleBridge is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data it uses

ArticleBridge asks Google for one permission: drive.file. With it, ArticleBridge can create files in the owner's Google Drive and read, update or move to trash only the files it created. It cannot see any other file in the owner's Drive, and it does not use Gmail, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service.

How the data is used

ArticleBridge uploads text notes that the owner has reviewed and approved to a private folder in the owner's own Drive, and checks that each upload arrived intact. It does nothing else with Google data.

Storage and sharing

The Google sign-in is stored only on the owner's own computer, readable only by the owner. ArticleBridge has no server and sends no data to anyone other than Google. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising, and no data is used to train AI models.

Removing access

The owner can remove ArticleBridge's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already uploaded stay in the owner's Drive until the owner deletes them.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email address shown on ArticleBridge's Google sign-in screen.
