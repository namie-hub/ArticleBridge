ArticleBridge

A personal tool, used only by its owner.

ArticleBridge turns articles that its owner has saved on their own computer into tidy text notes. After the owner reviews and approves each note, ArticleBridge uploads that note to a private folder in the owner's own Google Drive.

It uses Google's drive.file permission, so it can only see and change files it created itself. It cannot read anything else in the owner's Drive.

ArticleBridge is not offered to the public and has no other users.
